How to play

Command view

1–6 or click a tower, then click a tile to build. Shift keeps building.

Click a tower to upgrade (U) or sell (X).

WASD / right-drag to pan, wheel to zoom. N calls the next wave early for bonus credits.

Uplink — your ultimate

Kills charge the Uplink. When it is full press Space to drop onto a tower as Kestrel.

LMB arrows · RMB Rail Shot · Q Arrow Rain · E Singularity.

Aim at another tower and press F to teleport. Space ends early and refunds charge.

Cards & the bow

Every level you pick one of three cards: new tower blueprints, tower buffs, hero powers or economy.

Your bow evolves every 4 waves — pierce, extra arrows, explosions, chain lightning.

Survive 20 waves to win. A Colossus arrives every 5th wave.
