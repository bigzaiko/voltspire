How to play

Command view

Pick a tower from the bar (1 to 9, 0, T), then click a free hex to build. Shift keeps building.

Click a tower to upgrade (U) or sell (X). The tags over the spawn gates show what the next wave sends down each road.

WASD or right-drag to pan, wheel to zoom. Q E or middle-drag turns the view, R resets it.

N calls the next wave early for bonus credits. G switches the game speed, P or Esc pauses. F3 shows frame and mouse diagnostics.

Tap a tower in the bar, then tap a hex. Hold and drag to move the preview first. Tap a tower on the board to upgrade or sell it.

Drag with one finger to pan. Two fingers either pinch to zoom or twist to turn the view. The tags over the spawn gates show what the next wave sends down each road.

Uplink

Kills charge the Uplink. When it is full press Spacetap the Uplink button to stand on an Uplink Spire as Kestrel. She can only use Spires, so keep at least one.

Hold LMB to draw the bow, aim, let go to loose. A full draw hits hardest. RMB Rail Shot, Q Arrow Rain, E Singularity.

Aim at another Spire and press F, or shoot its beacon, to jump there. Space ends early and refunds charge.

Drag anywhere to aim. Hold Draw to pull the bow and let go to loose: a full draw hits hardest. The round buttons are your skills. Shoot the beacon of another Spire, or tap Jump, to move there.

Towers charge a special. Shoot the glowing node on top of a charged tower to fire it. The tower then reboots for a few seconds. Brutes and the Colossus have weak points that take extra arrow damage.

Uplink mode plays the whole run from the Spire. B or a tower key opens build mode: the crosshair marks a hex, LMB builds there, the wheel picks the tower, X sells and U upgrades the tower it marks (with nothing marked, U upgrades your own Spire), RMB or B closes it. A solo game slows down while it is open. Build a second Spire and jump to it to reach the rest of the grid.

Uplink mode plays the whole run from the Spire. Tap Build, pick a tower in the bar, aim at a hex and tap Place. Sell takes down the tower you aim at, Upgrade improves it (or your own Spire when you aim at no tower). Build a second Spire and jump to it to reach the rest of the grid.

Cards and the bow

Every level you pick one of three cards: tower blueprints, tower upgrades, hero powers or economy.

Your bow changes every 4 waves: pierce, extra arrows, explosions, chain lightning.

A campaign lasts 20, 25 or 30 waves depending on the threat level. A Colossus arrives every 5th wave. Survival has no last wave.

A machine that reaches the Heart stops and attacks it. Destroy it before it strikes and the core takes no damage.

Broodcarriers let mites loose that climb a tower and jam it: shoot the mites off. Late in a campaign the Titan lands. Only its weak points and tower specials hurt it, and the Uplink stays open while it stands.
