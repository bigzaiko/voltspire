Field manual

How to play

Goal Waves Towers Supply Cards Controls Icons

Goal

Guard the Heart

Machines march down the roads. Stop each one before it strikes the core.

More

A machine that reaches the Heart stops and attacks it. Destroy it before it strikes and the core takes no damage.

A campaign lasts 20, 25 or 30 waves, by threat level. Survival has no last wave.

Who comes when

New machines join as the waves go on. A Colossus comes every 5th wave.

Jammed or stunned

Violet ring: mites jam the tower, any hit knocks them off. Blue ring: the Titan's stun.

More

Broodcarriers let mites loose that climb a tower and jam it.

Late in a campaign the Titan lands and stuns every tower until it reboots. Its hull turns most tower fire: save the specials for it.

Build phase and waves

Time stands still

Between waves nothing moves: build in peace, then press Ntap Call wave.

More

Nothing is made and nothing moves until you call the wave, unless Auto waves calls for you.

G switches the game speed, P or Esc pauses.

Read the gate tags

Each spawn gate shows what the next wave sends down its road.

Upgrade or sell

ClickTap anything you built: U upgrades, X sells.

More

Towers go up to Mk3. Transfer Arms, mines, pumpjacks, plants and generators upgrade twice to work faster.

Towers

Ten towers, each with its own supply

The Pulse Turret is there from the start; the rest come as blueprint cards. Point atTap one to see its supply chain.

Pick its target

Select a tower and choose who it shoots (T).

More

Set all gives the mode to every tower of that kind (so does Shift-click), and new ones start with it. Shift+T steps back. A bracket marks the enemy the selected tower aims at.

Charge, then fire

Right-clickHold or double-tap a charged tower to fire its special.

More

It does not have to be selected (or select it and press Space) (or tap it and use Fire special). The tower reboots for a few seconds after.

Mk3 ammo switch

Two towers can switch to a stronger supply at Mk3.

More

Upgrading never changes what a tower eats. The card puts a switch into each Mk3's panel: convert a tower once its new supply line stands.

Supply

Towers fire only what they are fed

Mine, arm, turret in a line is a whole supply line.

More

A tower starts empty and keeps one item in hand. A Mine digs about one ore every 3 seconds, enough for one Pulse Turret (point at a deposit to see what it is) (tap a deposit to see what it is).

The one exception: a supply crate feeds the towers built right next to it, until it runs out.

Arms make every hand-off

An arm takes from the hex behind it and puts into the hex in front.

More

It only picks what its target takes right now, so nothing jams. R turns it; dragging lays a line of arms.Turn changes its direction; hold its preview and drag to lay a line.

ClickTap an arm to give it a filter: one kind of item, to pull it off a mixed belt.

Belts only carry

A belt never feeds a tower: an arm takes off what its tower needs.

More

Belts come with the Conveyor Belts card, always among the first offers. One belt can pass a row of towers. A belt pointing into another merges onto it.

Pipes and power

A pipe feeds what it touches. Power reaches 2 tiles round a Generator or Pole.

More

Poles wire up to 5 tiles apart, across roads too. The Power gauge shows what the grids need against what they have.

Rings show what will run

Green: supplied. Broken rust ring: something is missing.

More

Between waves a rust ring with a hologram of the missing item marks what will run short; clicktap it to see which hand-off is missing.

While you place something, dashes flow along its route and brackets close on what it would feed. Hold V for the green rings, or keep them on withKeep the green rings on with the hexagon button beside Auto waves.

The Factory bar

Every building is there from the start. A hatched one names the card that unlocks it.

Cards

Level up: pick one of three

Blueprints bring towers, production cards bring buildings, upgrades make them stronger.

More

Cards come as tower blueprints, production, tower upgrades, factory upgrades and economy. A blueprint unlocks only its tower; it names the production cards its supply still waits for, and those come into the next offers.

Cards build on cards

A card is offered once the cards it needs are yours.

Plan with Paths

On the level-up screen, Paths (4) shows what unlocks what.

Report a bug

      

      
This opens GitHub in a new tab. Nothing is sent until you submit the issue there, and the issue is public. You need a GitHub account.

      
It holds what you write, the run (map, wave, settings), your browser, OS and GPU names, recent game events and console messages (web addresses cut to the site), and the game state (towers, buildings, enemies, cards: nothing else). Not your saved progress, your music list or any account.
