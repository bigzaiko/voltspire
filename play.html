How to play

Command view

Pick a tower or a factory building from the bar (number keys; F switches between Towers and Factory), then click a free hex to build. Shift keeps building.

Click something you built to upgrade (U) or sell (X) it: towers, and mines, pumpjacks, plants and generators too. T steps a selected tower through its targeting modes (Shift+T backwards). The tags over the spawn gates show what the next wave sends down each road.

WASD or right-drag to pan, wheel to zoom. Q E or middle-drag turns the view, Home resets it.

N calls the next wave. Between waves time stands still: build, nothing is made and nothing moves until you call (or Auto waves calls for you). G switches the game speed, P or Esc pauses.

Tap a building in the bar, then tap a hex. Hold and drag to move the preview first (a belt follows your finger). Tap something on the board to upgrade or sell it.

Drag with one finger to pan. Two fingers either pinch to zoom or twist to turn the view. Between waves time stands still until you tap Call wave, unless you switch Auto waves on.

Supplying towers

A tower fires only what it is fed. It starts empty and keeps just one item in hand, pulling the next off its belt or pipe as it fires. Supply crates on the deck feed any tower built right next to them, but a crate runs out: every tower needs a supply line of its own in the end.

A Mine digs about one ore every 3 seconds, enough for one Pulse Turret; build it right next to the turret or run a belt between them (point attap a deposit to see what it is). Drag to lay a line of belts; R turns the belt you are placing.Hold and drag to lay belts; Turn changes their direction.

Nothing needs pointing: a building hands what it makes to a neighbour that needs it, or to a belt beside it when something further down that belt needs it. Towers and buildings take what they need from a belt that points at them or runs past them, so one belt can feed a whole row of towers. A belt pointing into another merges onto it.

Between waves a broken rust ring under a tower or building, with a hologram of the missing item over it, means it will lack something when the wave starts; clicktap it to see what is missing and what supplies it. The Power gauge shows what the grids need against what they have.

While you place something, a green ring marks everything that will be supplied, dashes flow along the route the new piece would get, and brackets close on what it would feed or power. Hold V to see the green rings at any time between waves, or switch them on for good with the hexagon button beside Auto waves.The hexagon button beside Auto waves keeps the green rings on between waves.

Each blueprint card unlocks a tower together with the buildings its supply needs, one step at a time. The Tunnels card adds Belt and Pipe Tunnels that pass up to 4 tiles under roads, towers and buildings. Mines, pumpjacks, plants and generators upgrade twice to work faster.

Cards, specials and the machines

Every level you pick one of three cards: tower blueprints, tower upgrades, factory upgrades or economy.

Towers charge a special. Select a charged tower and press Space or Fire special. The tower then reboots for a few seconds.

A campaign lasts 20, 25 or 30 waves depending on the threat level. A Colossus arrives every 5th wave. Survival has no last wave. A machine that reaches the Heart stops and attacks it. Destroy it before it strikes and the core takes no damage.

Every tower that picks a target can be told which: select it and choose First, Last, Strongest, Weakest, Fastest or Slowest in its panel (T steps through them). Set all gives the mode to every tower of that kind, as does a Shift-click on a mode, and new towers of the kind start with it. A bracket marks the enemy the selected tower is aiming at.

Broodcarriers let mites loose that climb a tower and jam it: a violet ring crackles round its base with a mite over it, and any hit knocks them off. Late in a campaign the Titan lands and stuns every tower (a cold blue ring and a bolt until it reboots): its hull turns most tower fire, so save the specials for it.
