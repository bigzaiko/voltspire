How a run plays

Build in the calm. Hold in the storm.

BEATSPIRE is a tower defense and a small factory game on the same board. The towers hold the road, the factory keeps them loaded, and the cards you draw decide how both of them grow.

01

Time stands still while you build

No wave starts until you call it. Between waves nothing moves and nothing is made, so there is no clock on your planning. The board tells you, before you commit, whether every tower will be fed when the shooting starts.

  • Green ring It will get everything it needs. Hold V to see the rings at any time between waves.
  • Broken rust ring It will lack something. The hologram over it shows what; its panel says what would supply it.
  • Route pulse While you place a piece, dashes run along the route it would get, and brackets close on what it would feed or power.
02

Call the wave

Press N and everything starts at once: mines dig, belts roll, the Static comes through the gates. Tags over the gates have already told you what each road gets.

A tower starts empty and keeps one item in hand, pulling the next off its belt or pipe as it fires. One that runs dry stops and shows what it is missing. Kills and cleared waves pay credits; a machine that reaches the Heart stops to attack it, and you can still destroy it before it strikes.

  • Every tower charges a special. Select it and fire with Space; the tower reboots afterwards, so pick the moment
  • Auto waves is a switch for when the factory runs itself, and G doubles the game speed
03

Level up, pick one of three

Each level deals you three cards out of 63. A blueprint unlocks a tower together with every building its supply needs, tier by tier, so each new tower adds one step to the factory you already run.

  • Tower cards sharpen the towers you rely on: Twin Barrels, Chain Reaction, Armour Breaker
  • Factory cards speed up mines, belts and plants, stretch every ammunition item, or unlock tunnels
  • Upgrades cost credits: towers go to Mk3 (the heavy ones for building materials too), and mines, pumpjacks, plants and generators work 1.6 times and 2.3 times as fast at Mk2 and Mk3
04

Then the big ones arrive

A Colossus closes every fifth wave. From wave 8 a Gyre rolls in between them, shrugging off hits while its rim spins. And once, near the end of a campaign, the Titan leaps out of the city onto your gate, stuns every tower and walks for the Heart.

Hold out for 20 waves on Recruit, 25 on Veteran or 30 on Nightmare and the district is yours. Then keep going if you like: after a win there is no last wave.

01

Four deposits on every map

Point at a patch to see what it is. The game lays them out by rule around the roads: iron and coal within a short belt of your first supply crate, at least one patch of copper and of oil on every map, the oil far from the core.

  • The deposit cards are built by the page's script.
02

Dig it up

Slow on purpose: one Mine keeps one Pulse Turret firing. More guns mean more mines, or better ones.

    03

    Power it

    One grid per line of poles. The Power gauge shows what it needs against what it has.

      04

      Move it

      Nothing has an output side and nothing needs pointing. A building hands what it makes to a neighbour that needs it, or to a belt beside it when something further down that belt needs it, so a belt never fills with things nobody wants.

        05

        Make it

        Six plants, each with one job. They arrive with the blueprint of the tower that needs them, and upgrade twice to keep up with it.

          06

          Feed the guns

          Pick a tower to see what it lives on, from the deposit to the muzzle.

          The supply-chain diagram is drawn by the page's script. In short: Pulse Turrets fire iron ore; Cryo, Arc and Beacon run on power; Firewall Pylons burn crude oil; Railguns fire copper plates; Mortars fire shells; Lances and Graviton Wells run on energy cells; Missile Batteries fire missile packs.

          Nyx-9 2187

          One core left. One factory to hold it.

          The megacity of Nyx-9 has gone dark district by district. The Static, a rogue machine swarm that answers to no one, has taken every power core but one. Now it is marching on the last: the Heart.

          You hold the district grid from the command deck. There are no supply convoys any more: what the towers fire, you dig out of the ground under them and build yourself.

          Your war

          Pick the ground. Set the stakes.

          38 pre-made maps in three tiers run from one long switchback to three roads closing on the core at once, some climbing ramps and crossing each other on bridges. Three difficulties change how hard the Static hits, how much your core can take and how long the run is.

          20-30 waves

          Campaign

          Hold the Heart for 20 waves on Recruit, 25 on Veteran or 30 on Nightmare. The Titan comes once, near the end. After a win you can keep going.

          No last wave

          Survival

          The machines keep getting tougher until the core falls, and the Titan keeps coming back. Your best wave per map and difficulty is saved in your browser.

          A map from a seed

          Generated

          Let the game draw the district: one to three roads with ramps and bridges, deposits and supply crates laid out by rule. Same seed, same map, so a good one can be passed on.

          Editor

          Draw your own district

          Draw up to three roads on the hex grid, raise them into ramps and bridges, place the core, wreckage and starting towers, and set credits and core integrity. Supply crates take their places beside the roads by themselves.

          • Resources tool Paint iron, copper, coal and oil yourself, or leave them automatic and let the rules lay them out
          • It checks your map The editor shows the deposits and crates a run would start with, and warns when a resource is missing or the iron lies out of belt reach of a crate

          Controls

          Keyboard, mouse or touch

          Build

          1-0
          Pick a tower or factory building, then click a free hex to build. Hold Shift to keep building.
          F
          Switch the build bar between Towers and Factory.
          R
          Turn the belt or tunnel you are placing or have selected. Drag to lay a line of belts or pipes.
          U X
          Upgrade or sell what you have selected: towers, and mines, pumpjacks, plants and generators too.
          V
          Hold to see the green supply rings between waves. The hexagon button beside Auto waves keeps them on.

          Fight

          N
          Call the next wave. Until then, time stands still.
          Space
          Fire the special of the selected tower when it is charged.
          G
          Switch the game speed between 1x and 2x.
          Esc P
          Pause.

          Camera

          WASD
          Pan, or drag with the right mouse button. The wheel zooms.
          Q E
          Turn the view, or drag with the middle mouse button. Home resets it.

          Touch and extras

          Tap
          Tap a building in the bar, then a hex. Hold and drag to move the preview first; a belt follows your finger, and Turn changes its direction. Tap something on the board to upgrade or sell it.
          Drag Pinch
          One finger pans. Two fingers pinch to zoom or twist to turn the view.
          Music
          Five built-in tracks play as a playlist. Add YouTube or Spotify links of your own and the score goes quiet while they play.
          Effects
          Impact and beat pulses can be switched off, dimmed, recoloured or limited to tile borders, environment or towers.

          Safe by design

          Just the game

          • No account, no login. Open the page and play.
          • No ads, no tracking. No analytics, no third-party scripts. A YouTube or Spotify player only loads if you add a link to the playlist and press play.
          • Nothing stored on a server. Settings, records and maps stay in your own browser.
          • Open source. Every line is public and can be checked.
