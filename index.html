The game

Two games, one battlefield

BEATSPIRE moves between a commander's map and an archer's sightline. The cards you draw decide how both of them grow.

01

Command view

From above, the district is a board of hexes. Build towers on any free hex beside a road, upgrade each one twice, and sell what you no longer need. Roads can climb and cross each other on bridges. Tags over the spawn gates show what the next wave sends down each road.

  • You start with a few tower types and unlock the rest as blueprint cards
  • Call the next wave early for bonus credits
  • A Colossus arrives every fifth wave. Hold 20, 25 or 30 waves to win, then keep going if you like
02

Uplink

Kills charge your ultimate. When it is full, the camera drops to the top of an Uplink Spire and you are Kestrel, an archer in third person, for a short time. She can only stand on Spires, so every map starts with one and you can build more. Aim at another Spire to jump to it.

Each combat tower charges a special. Shoot the glowing power node on top of a charged tower to fire it. The tower then reboots for a few seconds. Brutes and the Colossus have weak points that take extra arrow damage.

  • LMBRapid arrows Hold to loose a steady stream of shots.
  • RMBRail Shot One heavy line that tears through the column.
  • QArrow Rain Saturate an area from above.
  • ESingularity A gravity well that drags the swarm together, then detonates.
  • FTeleport Jump to the Spire in your sights.
03

Cards

Each level you are dealt three upgrade cards and keep one. Tower blueprints open new builds, per-tower upgrades sharpen the ones you rely on, hero powers reshape Kestrel's kit, and economy cards pay for all of it.

The bow evolves on its own as the run goes on, every four waves, through five tiers.

  1. CarbonStandard arrows
  2. PulsePierces a target
  3. TempestExtra arrow
  4. NovaExplodes on impact
  5. SingularityChains lightning

Threat index

The Static

A machine swarm with no pilot and no off switch. It arrives in waves, and each wave is tougher than the last.

  • Grunt

    From wave 1

    The line infantry of the swarm. Weak alone, never alone.

  • Runner

    From wave 2

    Fragile and very fast. Slips past slow guns in packs.

  • Drone

    From wave 4 / Flying

    Flies over the lane, out of reach of mortars.

  • Aegis

    From wave 6 / Shielded

    Carries an energy shield you must break before the hull.

  • Brute

    From wave 8 / Armored

    Slow, plated, and costs three core integrity if it gets through. Has a weak point on its back.

  • Gyre

    Mini-boss / Spin guard

    A machine riding inside one armoured wheel. While the rim spins fast it shrugs off most hits; slow it, freeze it or focus your fire and the guard drops.

  • Broodcarrier

    From wave 6 / Jams towers

    Walks the road and looses swarms of mites that climb the nearest tower and jam it. Shoot the mites off, or kill the carrier.

  • Colossus

    Every 5th wave / Boss

    A walking siege engine. Let one reach the Heart and it takes half your core with it. Aim for its exposed core.

  • Titan

    Once, near the end / Finale

    Twice as tall as a Spire. It lands on the gate, wrecks everything on the road and stuns your towers. Towers only slow it; your shots on its weak points and your tower specials bring it down.

Nyx-9 2187

One core left. One archer on the wire.

The megacity of Nyx-9 has gone dark district by district. The Static, a rogue machine swarm that answers to no one, has taken every power core but one. Now it is marching on the last: the Heart.

You are Kestrel. You hold the district grid from the command deck, and when the towers are not enough you ride the Uplink down to a Spire and hold it by hand.

Your war

Pick the ground. Set the stakes.

Eight pre-made maps run from one long switchback to three roads closing on the core at once. Or let the game generate a map from a seed, with ramps and bridges. Three difficulties change how hard the Static hits, how much your core can take and how long the run is.

20 / 25 / 30 waves

Campaign

Hold the Heart through 20 waves on Recruit, 25 on Veteran or 30 on Nightmare. After a win you can keep going with no last wave. Or play it in Uplink mode: the whole run on the Spire, and you build from up there.

No last wave / Score by wave

Survival

No final wave. The machines keep getting tougher until the core falls. Starts on a generated map unless you pick another. Your best wave per map is saved in your browser.

Build / Save / Share

Map editor

Draw up to three roads on the hex grid, raise them into ramps and bridges, place the core, wreckage and starting towers, set credits and core integrity. Test it in one click, save it in your browser, and share it as a code.

Online

Bring one more

Two ways to play with a friend, both joined with a six-character room code and nothing else.

2 players / Together

Co-op

Defend one grid and one core together, with shared credits and two archers. Each of you charges your own Uplink, so one can hold a Spire while the other keeps building.

1 v 1 / Against

Versus

Each of you defends your own grid. Spend credits to send squads at your rival: Runner Packs, Drone Wings, Aegis Trios and Brute Pairs. The last core standing wins.

Room code

K7X2QF

One player opens a room and reads out the code. The other types it in. The two browsers then connect directly, peer-to-peer.

Controls

Keyboard, mouse or touch

Command view

1-0 T

U X

WASD
Pan the camera, or drag with the right mouse button. Wheel zooms.
N
Call the next wave early.
Space
Uplink to the nearest Spire when the charge is full.
Esc P

Uplink

Mouse
Aim. Hold the left button to shoot. Hit a glowing power node to fire that tower's special.
RMB Q E
Rail Shot, Arrow Rain, Singularity.
F
Jump to the Spire you are aiming at.
Space
End the Uplink early and keep part of the charge.

Touch and extras

Touch
Tap to build and select, drag to pan, pinch to zoom. In the Uplink, drag to aim and use the on-screen buttons.
Music
Add YouTube or Spotify links to a playlist. The built-in score goes quiet while your music plays.
Effects
Impact and beat pulses can be switched off, dimmed, recoloured or limited to tile borders, environment or towers.

Safe by design

Just the game

  • No account, no login. Open the page and play.
  • No ads, no tracking. No analytics, no third-party scripts. A YouTube or Spotify player only loads if you add a link to the playlist and press play.
  • Nothing stored on a server. Settings, records and maps stay in your own browser.
  • Direct multiplayer. A match connects the two browsers to each other, so only share a room code with someone you trust.
  • Open source. Every line is public and can be checked.
