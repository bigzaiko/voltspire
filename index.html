The game

Two games, one battlefield

VOLTSPIRE moves between a commander's map and an archer's sightline. The cards you draw decide how both of them grow.

01

Command view

From above, the district is a board. Place towers on any free tile beside the lane, upgrade each one twice, and sell what no longer earns its square. Six tower types cover every job: rapid fire, slows, chain lightning, long-range blasts, piercing beams and support auras.

  • Start with the Pulse Turret and Cryo Emitter; earn the other four as blueprints
  • Call the next wave early for bonus credits
  • A Colossus arrives every fifth wave; survive twenty to win, then push on in endless mode
02

Uplink

Every kill charges your ultimate. When it is full, the camera drops from the sky to a tower top and you are Kestrel: an archer in third person with a few seconds to turn the wave. Aim at another tower and teleport to it to follow the fight.

  • LMBRapid arrows Hold to loose a steady stream of shots.
  • RMBRail Shot One heavy line that tears through the column.
  • QArrow Rain Saturate an area from above.
  • ESingularity A gravity well that drags the swarm together, then detonates.
  • FTeleport Jump to the tower in your sights.
03

Cards

Each level you are dealt three upgrade cards and keep one. Tower blueprints open new builds, per-tower upgrades sharpen the ones you rely on, hero powers reshape Kestrel's kit, and economy cards pay for all of it.

The bow evolves on its own as the run goes on, every four waves, through five tiers.

  1. CarbonStandard arrows
  2. PulsePierces a target
  3. TempestExtra arrow
  4. NovaExplodes on impact
  5. SingularityChains lightning

Arsenal

Tower roster

Six machines, six jobs. Each upgrades through three levels, and each has cards of its own.

  • Rapid fire50 cr

    Pulse Turret

    Rapid homing bolts. Cheap, reliable, and the backbone of every opening.

  • Crowd control70 cr

    Cryo Emitter

    Chills a whole group and slows it, holding the column in everyone else's range.

  • Chain damage110 cr

    Arc Pylon

    Lightning that jumps from enemy to enemy. The tighter the pack, the better it pays.

  • Artillery140 cr

    Plasma Mortar

    Long-range area blasts over the rooftops. Cannot hit flyers or anything at its feet.

  • Heavy sniper190 cr

    Railgun

    A slow, piercing beam with the longest reach on the grid. It picks the toughest target.

  • Support120 cr

    Signal Beacon

    Fires nothing. Boosts the damage and speed of every tower standing near it.

Threat index

The Static

A machine swarm with no pilot and no off switch. It arrives in waves, and each wave is tougher than the last.

  • Grunt

    From wave 1

    The line infantry of the swarm. Weak alone, never alone.

  • Runner

    From wave 2

    Fragile and very fast. Slips past slow guns in packs.

  • Drone

    From wave 4 · Flying

    Flies over the lane, out of reach of mortars.

  • Aegis

    From wave 6 · Shielded

    Carries an energy shield you must break before the hull.

  • Brute

    From wave 8 · Armored

    Slow, plated, and costs three core integrity if it gets through.

  • Colossus

    Every 5th wave · Boss

    A walking siege engine. Let one reach the Heart and it takes half your core with it.

Nyx-9 2187

One core left. One archer on the wire.

The megacity of Nyx-9 has gone dark district by district. The Static, a rogue machine swarm that answers to no one, has taken every power core but one. Now it is marching on the last: the Heart.

You are Kestrel. You hold the district grid from the command deck, and when the towers are not enough you ride the Uplink down to the rooftops and hold it by hand.

Online

Bring one more

Two ways to play with a friend, both joined with a six-character room code and nothing else.

2 players · Together

Co-op

Defend one grid and one core together, with shared credits and two archers. Each of you charges your own Uplink, so one can hold the rooftops while the other keeps building.

1 v 1 · Against

Versus

Each of you defends your own grid. Spend credits to send squads at your rival: Runner Packs, Drone Wings, Aegis Trios and Brute Pairs. The last core standing wins.

Room code

K7X2QF

One player opens a room and reads out the code. The other types it in. The two browsers then connect directly, peer-to-peer.
