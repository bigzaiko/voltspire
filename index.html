Build the grid. Become the storm.

Defend the last power core of Nyx-9 on a hex grid. Build towers beside the roads, then keep them firing. The game

Defend the road. Feed the guns.

BEATSPIRE is a tower defense and a small factory game on the same board. The towers hold the road, the factory keeps them supplied, and the cards you draw decide how both of them grow.

01

Command view

From above, the district is a board of hexes. Build towers on any free hex beside a road, upgrade each one twice, and sell what you no longer need. Roads can climb and cross each other on bridges. Tags over the spawn gates show what the next wave sends down each road.

  • You start with a few tower types and unlock the rest as blueprint cards
  • The stronger towers cost building materials from a Frame Works on top of credits
  • Waves start when you call them. Between waves time stands still: build, plan, and see what each tower will get. Auto waves is a switch
  • A Colossus arrives every fifth wave. Hold 20, 25 or 30 waves to win, then keep going if you like
02

The factory

A tower fires only what it is fed, and it starts empty. It keeps one item in hand and pulls the next off its belt as it fires. Supply crates on the deck feed a tower built right next to them, but a crate runs out. The Pulse Turret runs on iron ore, so the first supply line is one Mine and maybe a belt. Mines and plants work slowly; upgrade them twice to work faster.

Between waves the factory stands still with everything else. A marker over each tower and building says whether it will get what it needs when the wave starts, and its panel says what is missing.

Each blueprint card unlocks a tower together with the buildings its supply needs, a step at a time: a Generator for the Cryo Emitter and the Arc Pylon, a Pumpjack and pipes for the Firewall Pylon, a Smelter for the Railgun's copper plates, a Shell Press for the Mortar, a Cell Plant for the Lance, a Refinery and a Missile Works for the Missile Battery. At Mk3 the Pulse Turret switches to plasma shells and the Firewall Pylon to petrol.

  • BeltConveyor belts Drag to lay a line. Nothing needs pointing: buildings load a belt when something further down it needs what they make, and a belt feeds every tower it points at or runs past. Belts merge, and a Splitter deals items out to several lines.
  • PipePipes Pumpjacks fill them with crude oil, refineries with petrol.
  • PolePower lines Generators burn coal. Poles wire up to 5 tiles apart, also across roads, and power everything within 2 tiles.
  • TunnelTunnels A card that unlocks Belt and Pipe Tunnels: they pass up to 4 tiles under roads, towers and buildings.
03

Cards

Each level you are dealt three upgrade cards and keep one. Tower blueprints open new builds, per-tower upgrades sharpen the ones you rely on, factory cards speed up your mines, belts and machines, and economy cards pay for all of it.

Every tower charges a special. Select a charged tower and fire it: a Bullet Storm, a Flash Freeze, a Thunderhead, a Barrage. The tower reboots afterwards, so pick the moment.

Threat index

The Static

A machine swarm with no pilot and no off switch. It arrives in waves, and each wave is tougher than the last.

  • Grunt

    From wave 1

    The line infantry of the swarm. Weak alone, never alone.

  • Runner

    From wave 2

    Fragile and very fast. Slips past slow guns in packs.

  • Drone

    From wave 4 / Flying

    Flies over the lane, out of reach of mortars.

  • Aegis

    From wave 6 / Shielded

    Carries an energy shield you must break before the hull.

  • Brute

    From wave 8 / Armored

    Slow, plated, and costs three core integrity if it gets through. Railguns with Armour Breaker cut through it.

  • Gyre

    Mini-boss / Spin guard

    A machine riding inside one armoured wheel. While the rim spins fast it shrugs off most hits; slow it, freeze it or focus your fire and the guard drops.

  • Broodcarrier

    From wave 6 / Jams towers

    Walks the road and looses swarms of mites that climb the nearest tower and jam it. Cover your towers with each other, or kill the carrier first.

  • Colossus

    Every 5th wave / Boss

    A walking siege engine. Let one reach the Heart and it takes a quarter of your core with it. Save your specials for it.

  • Titan

    Once, near the end / Finale

    Towers over the whole district. It lands on the gate, wrecks everything on the road and stuns your towers. Its hull turns most tower fire; tower specials hit it at full strength.

Nyx-9 2187

One core left. One factory to hold it.

The megacity of Nyx-9 has gone dark district by district. The Static, a rogue machine swarm that answers to no one, has taken every power core but one. Now it is marching on the last: the Heart.

You hold the district grid from the command deck. There are no supply convoys any more: what the towers fire, you dig out of the ground under them and build yourself.

Your war

Pick the ground. Set the stakes.

38 pre-made maps run from one long switchback to three roads closing on the core at once. Or let the game generate a map from a seed, with ramps and bridges. Three difficulties change how hard the Static hits, how much your core can take and how long the run is.

20 / 25 / 30 waves

Campaign

Hold the Heart through 20 waves on Recruit, 25 on Veteran or 30 on Nightmare. After a win you can keep going with no last wave.

No last wave / Score by wave

Survival

No final wave. The machines keep getting tougher until the core falls. Starts on a generated map unless you pick another. Your best wave per map is saved in your browser.

Build / Save / Share

Map editor

Draw up to three roads on the hex grid, raise them into ramps and bridges, place the core, wreckage and starting towers, set credits and core integrity. Deposits are laid out on the free deck for you. Test it in one click, save it in your browser, and share it as a code.

Controls

Keyboard, mouse or touch

Command view

1-0
Pick a tower or factory building, then click a hex to build. Hold Shift to keep building.
F
Switch the build bar between towers and the factory.
R
Turn the belt or tunnel you are placing or have selected. Drag to lay a line of belts or pipes.
U X
Upgrade or sell the selected tower; upgrade or remove the selected factory building.
WASD
Pan the camera, or drag with the right mouse button. Wheel zooms.
N
Call the next wave. Until then, time stands still.
Space
Fire the special of the selected tower when it is charged.
Esc P
Pause.

Touch and extras

Touch
Tap to build and select, drag to pan, pinch to zoom. Hold and drag to lay belts; the Turn button changes their direction.
Music
Add YouTube or Spotify links to a playlist. The built-in score goes quiet while your music plays.
Effects
Impact and beat pulses can be switched off, dimmed, recoloured or limited to tile borders, environment or towers.

Safe by design

Just the game

  • No account, no login. Open the page and play.
  • No ads, no tracking. No analytics, no third-party scripts. A YouTube or Spotify player only loads if you add a link to the playlist and press play.
  • Nothing stored on a server. Settings, records and maps stay in your own browser.
  • Open source. Every line is public and can be checked.
